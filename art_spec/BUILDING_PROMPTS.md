# 建筑提示词（贴合角色全家福，全新设计）

用法：只附**角色全家福**一张图（定画风），复制一段发给生图 AI，一段出一张，按标题的文件名保存，打包发我。

## 第 0 步：先出一张建筑全家福（推荐）

先出这张把建筑画风定下来，满意了以后，单张都**同时附角色全家福 + 这张建筑全家福**，再加一句 `the same building as in the building lineup`，这样每栋楼风格、大小都统一。

```
A lineup of seven buildings for a medieval strategy game, in exactly the same art style as the attached character lineup: same thick dark grey-brown outline, flat colors with one darker shade tone, same muted desaturated earthy palette (warm grey stone, taupe and brown wood, slate blue, dull red, olive green), chunky simple rounded shapes, slightly squat cartoonish proportions like the characters, very little detail. Seen from above at about 45 degrees (3/4 top-down view), front facing the viewer. Same scale, evenly spaced in one row on a plain white background: a stone castle keep with two round towers and blue cone roofs; a stone and timber barracks with a weapon rack; a wooden storage barn with crates and barrels; a stone forge with a brick chimney and glowing furnace; an open timber carpenter's shed with planks; a construction site with wooden scaffolding; a slime monster nest mound with a dark cave mouth. No ground, no shadows, no characters, no text.
```

## 小提示

- 太亮、太细、有渐变：补一句 `keep the same flat muted style as the character lineup`
- 变成正面平视：补一句 `seen from above at about 45 degrees`
- 一张一个建筑，不要地面、草地、影子和人物
